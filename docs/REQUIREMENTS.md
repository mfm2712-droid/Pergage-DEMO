# Pergage requirements trace

Source: `Pergage website answers for Mark 27 10 26.pdf`.

## Implemented as provisional scaffolding

- Tagline: “Engagement, engineered”.
- Warm, credible, precise UK-English tone.
- Organisational and Individual Readiness scorecard entry points.
- Results remain on screen without contact details.
- Calculation happens in the server route, not in browser page code.
- Public contact email, LinkedIn link and UK location.
- 30-minute call positioning with the stated availability.
- Homepage services: assessment, scorecards, team diagnostics, training/coaching and ongoing improvement.

## Not final and deliberately configurable

Paul’s document says the final questions, scoring, weighting and result copy are not yet ready. The prototype therefore uses five clearly provisional questions per scorecard and a simple average only to prove the flow. These must be replaced before launch.

## Safety and ownership boundaries

- No domains, DNS, production hosting, purchases or external account changes were made.
- No analytics or visitor statistics are enabled.
- No lead capture, email follow-up or data storage is enabled.
- The final infrastructure should be created under Pergage Ltd ownership, with developer access delegated separately.
